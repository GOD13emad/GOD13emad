## Authorship, privacy and evidence level

These eight entries document areas of **technical work present in the owner's project/research environment**; possession of a file does not conclusively establish sole authorship, external contractual delivery, intellectual-property release rights or a certified result. Collaborators, university/institutional rights and third-party research sources must be respected. Some OneDrive academic directories contain teaching resources and unrelated published literature, which are *references*, not personal publications.

The local source audit also found additional **unreviewed** categories such as special-purpose COMSOL models, AI/game experiments, internal university/industry research records, older theses and historical CVs. They are deliberately excluded from this count until status, rights and technical outputs are independently verified. **No login credentials, private PDF/Word files, photographs, source-video assets or proprietary engineering designs were uploaded to this portfolio.**
